Computation in the service of real-world problems

D-AMSC Building — IIT Roorkee

With the growing complexities of real-world problems, knowledge of efficient computational techniques has become not just desirable but also necessary in cases where traditional methods fall short. At the Department of Applied Mathematics and Scientific Computing (D-AMSC), the aim is to groom students in such methods and prepare them to take on challenging real-life problems arising across science and multidisciplinary fields. Faculty at D-AMSC work across Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Machine Learning, Numerical Optimization, Mathematical Modelling, Numerical Differential Equations, and Related Computational Methods.

01 Academic programmes and Courses

Academic programmes

M.Tech.

Applied Mathematics & Scientific Computing  ·  Pre-final year: 11  ·  Final year: 7 + 2(M.Tech. by Research)

The Master’s programme in Applied Mathematics and Scientific Computing (M.Tech.) provides advanced, interdisciplinary training at the interface of mathematics, computation, and Artificial Intelligence. The programme builds a strong foundation in numerical methods, probability, algorithms, optimization, data science and analysis, and software development, while introducing students to contemporary areas such as deep learning, explainable AI, and evolutionary computation. Through project-based learning and practical applications, the M.Tech. equips students to formulate and solve complex computational problems, develop efficient algorithms, and apply mathematical and AI-driven approaches to real-world challenges.

MS(By Research)

Applied Mathematics & Scientific Computing  ·  Students: 2(Final year)

The MS (By Research) programme is a research-oriented Master’s degree in Applied Mathematics and Scientific Computing, in which students work closely with a faculty supervisor on an in-depth thesis. It emphasises mathematical modelling, computational methods, and AI-driven approaches, and prepares students for doctoral studies and research careers.

Ph.D.

Scholars in progress: 55

The Doctoral Programme focuses on advanced research at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, optimization, mathematical modelling, and computational differential equations. Research spans diverse application domains, including healthcare, supply chain management, finance, and engineering systems, with areas such as medical image analysis, health informatics, and network performance. The programme integrates mathematical theory, computational techniques, and data-driven approaches to address complex real-world problems and develop innovative, impactful solutions.
